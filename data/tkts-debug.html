TKTS Accessibility Policy

Our TKTS Patron Services Representatives and ticket sellers are happy to assist people with disabilities. We can assist people with mobility, vision and auditory concerns in a variety of ways. Let us know if you have questions about what accommodations are available at the TKTS Booth as well as the theatre you’re visiting.

Helpful Tips

If you have any questions or need help while at the booth, find one of our TKTS Patron Services Representatives! They are available to help you with whatever you need and can be spotted wearing red TKTS shirts or jackets.

Payment Info

All TKTS locations accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover cards, cash and TKTS eGift Cards. A handful of Off-Broadway shows require cash only. All tickets are subject to a $8 per ticket service charge, which helps support other TDF services and programs. Additional non-TDF service charges may apply to some tickets.

Fast Pass

TKTS 7-Day Fast Pass allows TKTS patrons to return to the Times Square ticket booth within seven days of their TKTS purchase and walk up to the Fast Pass lane with their TKTS ticket stubs, thereby avoiding the line.

Why Should I Get My Last-Minute Discount Tickets at TKTS?

Everyone knows that purchasing tickets at TKTS is the best way to get affordable tickets to Broadway and Off-Broadway shows. TKTS has been in Times Square for more than 50 years, offering the widest selection of discount tickets. When you purchase tickets at TKTS, you know your seat locations and know exactly what you are paying for.

What you may not know is that TKTS is part of a larger entity, TDF, a not-for-profit organization. The $8 per-ticket service fee (significantly lower than online sales outlets) doesn’t just cover the costs of operating TKTS. It supports a host of programs that bring theatre to many people beyond those waiting in line.

Whether you’re a high school or middle school student who has never attended a show; or you have a physical disability such as a mobility issue, hearing or vision loss; or someone in your family is on the autism spectrum, TDF has programs and services to break the barriers to attending the theatre. So thank you for your loyal patronage at TKTS and remember, you’re not only on your way to a show, but you are also helping someone else have that memorable experience.

How many tickets can I buy?

There is a limit of six tickets per person at TKTS, but if you need more, don’t worry! Just have more than one person with you, or speak with a Patron Services Representative, who will assist you in getting the seats.
